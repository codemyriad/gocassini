### Added
- A recording can carry tags. A tag such as `hiring` can go on the whole meeting or on a stretch of it, and the marks live inside the recording's `.opus` file, so a meeting keeps its tags when it is downloaded or shared. The portable format and its schema describe them, and a reader that does not understand them still opens the recording normally.
- `cassini meetings tags` lists the tags on the meetings you can read. `cassini meetings annotations <id>` shows one meeting's marks, and `cassini meetings annotate <id> --ops <file>` adds or removes them in one batch. Every mark records the Nextcloud account that made it, and whether a person or an agent made it, so an agent's whole run can be undone in one step.
- `cassini meetings search` and `cassini meetings list` take `--tag` to look only at meetings with that tag. Against a Cassini app that does not offer tags, they fail instead of silently returning every meeting.
- `cassini inspect` reports a recording's tags: how many there are, and whether they were made against this recording's audio.

### Security
- A search term or tag no longer appears in the CLI's error output when the connection to Nextcloud fails before it answers.
- Tags travel with a recording. Anyone you share a tagged recording with can see who tagged what, by Nextcloud user id.
