### Removed
- **The Setup tab and its wizard are gone.** Cassini no longer opens on a mode question that blocks every recording until somebody answers it. A link to `#surface=setup` now lands on Browse.

### Added
- **One dialog, once per install.** The first administrator to open a fresh Cassini is told who will see recordings, and given one button that creates the `cassini` Nextcloud account the recordings are kept in. Nextcloud asks them to confirm their password, as it always did, and Cassini never sees it. The account's password is generated in the browser, set, and discarded: nothing signs in as the account with it, and "Set a password" is there for the rare day somebody needs to. The dialog is recorded as seen by the install, not by the browser, so the second administrator does not meet it again.
- **The meeting list says who can see the recordings in it**, beside the meeting count, for every user: "Visible to anyone with a Nextcloud account" or "Visible to meeting participants", each with the one sentence that explains it. It is the same for administrators and everybody else, so the two can never disagree about what is true.

### Changed
- **Nobody is told an administrator has to decide where recordings are kept**, because nothing waits for that decision any more. Someone who is not an administrator sees a notice only when something is genuinely broken, and the words for that are unchanged.
