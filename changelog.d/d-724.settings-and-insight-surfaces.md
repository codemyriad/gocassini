### Fixed
- Registering an AI provider is now the whole of the setup an insight needs. Before this, an administrator could save an endpoint and still be told "No AI endpoint is available" everywhere in the app, because an insight only resolved an endpoint through a pipeline step somebody had also switched on. An insight now resolves, in order: the endpoint its own step names, then the summary step's, then any registered provider — and the operator hands that resolved endpoint to the run, so what the settings page says and what the run receives cannot disagree. The rule that follows is stated in the AI providers panel and in `docs/privacy.md`: every endpoint you register is one an insight may reach, and removing them all is how insights are switched off. Summarising stays a separate opt-in that runs on every recording.
- An insight document no longer opens with a paragraph of YAML. The recorder writes its provenance — which meetings, which prompt version and hash, which model — as front matter at the top of the file, and markdown renders `---` as a horizontal rule, so twenty quoted keys arrived on screen above the answer somebody asked for. The provenance is still in the file, and the panel shows the same facts in its header and its "This run" list.

### Added
- **Pick the AI endpoint and model when you create an insight.** Prepare now offers a Provider and a Model beside the template, defaulting to the first configured endpoint and its own default model. It is offered to **everyone**, not only administrators: which of the configured endpoints sees your transcripts is the asker's decision, so `GET operator/ai/providers` is a USER route carrying ids and display names — the base URL, the key and the request bounds stay administrator-only. A retry re-runs what you asked for, falling back to the deployment's endpoint only if the one you chose has since been removed. **This adds a route, so the release that ships it must bump `<version>` in `appinfo/info.xml`** — AppAPI only rewrites an app's route table on a version change.
- **"Ask your own question"**, the freeform insight template. Pick it in Prepare, type a question, and the answer comes back in three parts: the answer itself in at most three sentences, the evidence with who said what and when, and — never omitted — what these meetings do not answer. The machinery for a caller-supplied question already existed (`{{QUESTION}}`, `Workflow.TakesQuestion`); what was missing was a prompt carrying the placeholder. A template that takes a question is offered wherever one can be typed and withheld where one cannot: it is not offered as the publish pipeline's summary step, which runs unattended with nobody to ask.

### Changed
- **Registering your first AI endpoint now switches meeting summaries on**, pointed at it. An install that has just configured an endpoint and still publishes meetings without summaries has done the work and not got the feature. It fires once — on the save that takes a deployment from having no endpoint to having one — so an administrator who turns summarising off keeps it off, whatever they save afterwards. The AI providers page says so before you save, since it is a decision about what leaves your deployment.
- **AI providers** configures endpoints and nothing else. Adding one is a form rather than a hidden row of fields, a saved endpoint reports whether its model list actually came back (and says so plainly when it did not, since an endpoint with no `/models` route still answers completions), and the page says which endpoint an insight will actually reach.
- **Publish pipeline** is now the whole pipeline in the order it runs: detected hardware, transcription quality, summarisation, and the device override last. Summarisation moved here from AI providers — it is a step between the call ending and the meeting being published, and it was only living under endpoints because its endpoint is.
- The summary step names an **insight template** from the registry instead of asking for a workflow id as free text, and its model field is a combobox that loads the endpoint's models when you open it rather than behind a "Load models" button. Typing a model the list does not have is still accepted: the list is the endpoint's, not a limit on what you may send.
- The "this needs an AI provider" state is now the same compact card wherever it appears — the summarisation step, Insight templates, and the Prepare panel — with the button that fixes it. Anyone who is not an administrator still gets the explanation instead, because that panel would refuse them.
- **Create an insight** in the Prepare panel is the template you pick and what it will ask, in one box: choosing a template shows the question it puts to the meetings and the shape of the document that comes back, or a question box where the template takes one. It opens on a real template rather than on a row reading "this deployment's default", which named nothing and read as a setting instead of a choice.
- The Prepare panel no longer lists previous insight runs. It was a job console in a panel whose subject is the meetings you just picked, and a second place insights were listed — free to disagree with the browse catalogue behind it. The card now shows only the run it starts, which is feedback for a button press; every insight, that one included, is a card in the browse list as soon as it exists.
- The publish pipeline's summary step opens on **Meeting summary** — the template it actually runs — instead of a "Default (the one Cassini ships)" row standing in front of it.
- **Meetings / Insights** moved out of the list header into the rooms rail, under "Show", beside the other narrowing of the same archive, with a clearer gap between the two lists.
- A meeting's header now carries its name, room, date, length and speakers, and stays put while the transcript scrolls under it. The insights a meeting was used in read directly under its summary rather than in a strip below the whole transcript, and an insight's own header carries its room, date and the model that answered.
- Insight cards in the browse list are filled rather than outlined, so they read as a different kind of thing at a glance rather than only by the rule down their edge.

### Removed
- The Insights step in AI providers. It configured "run insights somewhere other than the summary endpoint" while looking like the switch that turns insights on, which it never was. The field is still persisted and served, so `PUT operator/settings/llm` can still point insights at a larger model.
- The Prepare panel no longer says that a meeting "does not record whether it has a summary". It is true of every meeting published before the catalog carried the field, it changes nothing about what the bundle contains, and it buried the one gap that does stop a bundle being assembled.
