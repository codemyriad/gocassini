### Added
- **Meetings now know which conversation they came from, and you can list and filter by it.** Until now a recording's room name was flattened into its title and the room's identity was discarded, so there was no way to ask for "the meetings from this room". Every recording made from here on carries its Talk room's id and display name in its portable `.opus` and in the published catalog, and the CLI gains two ways to use that: `cassini meetings rooms` lists the conversations your account has readable recordings from, and `cassini meetings list` accepts `--room`, `--from` and `--to` (all optional, and they combine — `--from 2026-08-01 --to 2026-08-31` means all of August). The agent skill for reading meetings now narrows by room and date before listing, instead of fetching the whole archive and guessing.

### Changed
- **Recordings published before this update can be given their room back, from the files themselves.** Run `./scripts/backfill-catalog-rooms.sh` once, by hand, on the host where the app container runs; it reports what it would change and writes nothing until you add `--apply`. It recovers each meeting's room *name* from its published recording. It cannot recover the room's id for those meetings — that was never written into the file and cannot be derived from it — and it deliberately does not invent one, since a made-up identifier would silently group meetings that were never in the same room. Those meetings are listed and filterable by name instead.
