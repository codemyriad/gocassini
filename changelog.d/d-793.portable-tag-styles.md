### Added
- Tag colours and icons are now saved in each recording's `cassini.annotations.v1` document. Downloaded recordings and the public viewer retain their tag appearance without an installation-local lookup.

### Changed
- Renaming or restyling a tag uses the existing visibility-scoped background rewrite flow. Only recordings the caller can read are updated; inaccessible recordings keep their saved appearance.

### Fixed
- Applying an existing tag to another recording now copies its visible colour and icon into the new recording, including when tagging a selection. Existing local styles remain unchanged.
