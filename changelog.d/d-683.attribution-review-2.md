### Fixed
- Speaker attribution now calibrates a participant's noise floor across all of their packet streams. A rotation or rejoin produces a fresh stream, and a short one carrying only ongoing speech had that speech as its own floor — so the real speaker measured as barely audible and a quiet track carrying only their bleed won the word by 20 dB.
- Words keep their attribution provenance through LLM readable cleanup, so summaries actually exclude probable crosstalk. Readable cleanup and summarisation normally share one configured model, so losing the flag there made the summary protection a no-op in exactly the ordinary path.
- Filtering no longer discards older segments that carry text but no word list.
