### Added
- The meeting list is organised by room. A rooms nav down the left lists every room the archive knows about with its meeting count, alongside "All meetings" and a "No room" group that keeps recordings without a room — an imported file, a simulated run, an old recording — visible rather than hidden.
- Meetings are grouped under month headings, and each row now shows its room, its date, its speaker count and how long it ran.

### Changed
- Opening a meeting slides it in over the list from the right, instead of replacing the list with a second column; on a phone it arrives as a bottom sheet. Close it with the × or Escape, and the list is still there, in the same place, with the same room selected.
- The search box moved to the top of the list and filters within the selected room. It still matches a meeting's name or date, and the room and search narrowings each show as a chip you can clear.
