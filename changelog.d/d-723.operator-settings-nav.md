### Changed
- Configuration lives inside the Operator surface again, behind a left nav, instead of in a Settings tab of its own. Two tabs remain — Browse and Operator — and Operator now opens on a nav with two groups: Console (Recordings) and Settings (AI providers, Publish pipeline, Insight templates). The panels themselves are unchanged; only where you reach them moved. Below 720px the nav is a dropdown, and each panel opens at its own top rather than wherever the previous one was scrolled to.
- A panel can be linked to directly: `#surface=operator&panel=endpoints` opens AI providers.
- The run console splits into a list and a detail pane based on how wide the console itself is, not how wide the browser window is. In Nextcloud the app sits beside Nextcloud's own navigation, so a window wide enough by the old measure could still squeeze the run detail into a couple of hundred pixels.

### Added
- An Insight templates row in the Operator nav. It has no template registry behind it yet and says so — nothing is listed until there is something real to list.
