### Added
- Transcripts now carry cross-track speaker attribution evidence. Every word records how far the loudest other participant's microphone sat above its own noise floor compared with the speaker's own, so crosstalk the decoder transcribed as speech becomes visible instead of shipping unmarked. Where a meeting's own level distribution shows an unambiguous crosstalk population, affected words are flagged `lowConfidenceSpeaker`; the canonical transcript keeps every word, and the evidence travels with them as `attributionGapDb`.
- Generated summaries now exclude words flagged as probable crosstalk. A reader can see and overrule a marked word in the transcript; a summary has no such reader, and a fabricated interjection there becomes a decision somebody supposedly made.
- Speech recognition now runs behind a backend interface, so a second engine can be added by registering a factory rather than by changing the pipeline. Select one with `CASSINI_STT_BACKEND`; the bundled sherpa-onnx decoder remains the default, and an unknown selection fails loudly rather than silently transcribing with a different engine.

### Changed
- `CASSINI_ATTRIBUTION_DROP=1` removes flagged crosstalk words from the transcript itself instead of only marking them, and `CASSINI_ATTRIBUTION_DISABLED=1` skips the measurement. Marking is the default: real crosstalk overlaps the level range genuine simultaneous speech occupies, so deleting canonical words on a threshold is opt-in.
