### Added
- Transcripts now carry cross-track speaker attribution evidence. Every word records how far the loudest other participant's microphone sat above its own noise floor compared with the speaker's own, so crosstalk that the decoder transcribed as speech becomes visible instead of shipping unmarked. Where a meeting's own level distribution shows an unambiguous crosstalk population, affected words are flagged `lowConfidenceSpeaker`; the words themselves are kept, and the evidence travels with them as `attributionGapDb`.
- Speech recognition now runs behind a backend interface, so a second engine can be added by registering a factory rather than by changing the pipeline. Select one with `CASSINI_STT_BACKEND`; the bundled sherpa-onnx decoder remains the default.

### Changed
- `CASSINI_ATTRIBUTION_DROP=1` removes flagged crosstalk words instead of only marking them, and `CASSINI_ATTRIBUTION_DISABLED=1` skips the measurement entirely. Marking is the default: real crosstalk overlaps the level range genuine simultaneous speech occupies, so deleting on a threshold is opt-in.
