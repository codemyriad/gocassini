### Added

- A run's detail now says how long the meeting took to process against its length, as in "12m · 4.8× real time", so you can tell whether your server keeps up. Each attempt shows how long each of its steps took.
- The run's own progress bar describes the meeting as it stands: Record, Build, Summary, Seal and Publish, each taken from the attempt that produced it, with its time. A summary written later no longer hides the build that transcribed the meeting, and a summary written during the build says so. An attempt in progress, or one that failed, shows on the steps it reached.
