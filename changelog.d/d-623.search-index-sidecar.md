### Added
- Cassini's meeting-transcript search index lives in its own `search.sqlite3` file beside the job database, and holds nothing that cannot be rebuilt from the published recordings. Deleting it is safe: it costs a rebuild and never touches recordings or job history. An upgrade that changes the index format rebuilds it automatically rather than refusing to start.
- Each meeting is indexed as it is published, against the recording that was actually delivered. Results point at transcript segments — the speaker, the words and the times the recording itself carries.
- `cassini-operator backfill-search` indexes meetings published before the index existed. It is safe to re-run and safe to interrupt, and it indexes a meeting only when the stored transcript matches the recording that was delivered.
- A meeting that cannot be indexed is recorded as such, with a reason, rather than passing for searched — so a search can report partial coverage instead of a confident "no match". Indexing never blocks or fails a publish.
