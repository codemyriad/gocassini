### Added
- Cassini's meeting-transcript search index lives in its own `search.sqlite3` file beside the job database, and holds nothing that cannot be rebuilt from the published recordings. Deleting it is safe: it costs a rebuild and never touches recordings or job history. An upgrade that changes the index format rebuilds it automatically rather than refusing to start.
- Search results point at transcript segments — the speaker, the words and the times the recording itself carries. An index rebuilt from published recordings alone falls back to coarser time-ranged results, and records which of the two it holds so a result never implies a precision it does not have.
