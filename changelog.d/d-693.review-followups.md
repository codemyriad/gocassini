### Fixed
- A quick back-and-forth is no longer folded into one turn. Rebuilding a speaker's turn now needs the person who spoke in between to have actually overlapped or touched it: if they said their piece in clean air and stopped, both people kept their own turn, in the order they spoke. Without that, five real turns of a fast exchange collapsed into one row with an answer rendered before the question it answered.
- Playback follows the paragraph it is reading. It used to follow whichever producer fragment was sounding, so during two people talking over each other the page smooth-scrolled between the pieces of the same rebuilt paragraph every few hundred milliseconds.
- The "probably crosstalk" warning is judged once over everything a turn actually said, instead of once per fragment. One flagged fragment no longer condemns a whole genuine turn, and a short reaction that is entirely flagged keeps its warning now that it renders as a chip.
