### Added
- The health checklist now reports how much of your archive search can actually read — "search can read 129 of 138 meetings" — so a partial answer is visible rather than looking like no matches.
- A shortfall nobody can act on is not counted as a gap: a recording where nobody spoke, and an older meeting search cannot index at all, are both reported without being held against your coverage. They stay visible in the wording; they just don't turn the row amber.
