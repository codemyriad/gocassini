### Added
- `cassini retag` can now rewrite a recording's title and speaker roster: `--title` sets the meeting title (mirrored into the player-facing `TITLE` tag), and `--speaker` relabels a speaker as `<id>=<label>`, or as a bare `<label>` when the file has exactly one speaker. Speaker ids never move, because the transcript words reference them. This is what a capture whose speaker is the Talk account that dialled in ("Demo visitor", "admin") rather than the person who actually spoke needs, and it does not re-transcribe or re-encode anything.
- `cassini meetings summarize --from-file <summary.md>` seals a summary somebody wrote instead of generating one. The built-in prompt assumes a working meeting between colleagues, which is the wrong shape for a recording that is not one — a conference talk, an interview, a lecture. No LLM endpoint is required with it, and the manifest records the summary as `provenance.meetingSummary.source = "authored"` with no model name, so an authored summary is never mistaken for a generated one.
