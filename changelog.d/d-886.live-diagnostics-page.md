### Added
- Cassini Live gains a diagnostics page for administrators, served by the cassini-live app at `…/proxy/live_transcription/diagnostics/` and linked from its settings page. It shows what the in-call diagnostic video shows — caption status, voices, seats, caption lag, two minutes of speaker lanes, the lag graph, admission decisions, memory and CPU budgets, and how a caption is made — both views at once, live, without joining a call or changing a deploy option. Speaker names appear only to an administrator who is a participant of that conversation; the app samples only while someone has the page open. The diagnostic video keeps working as before.
