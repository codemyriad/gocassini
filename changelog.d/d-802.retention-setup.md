### Added
- Administrators who have not saved retention settings are offered an initial review alongside recording access, using the same complete retention editor as Operator → Storage. The review overlays the Cassini scaffold with an internally scrollable body and fixed actions. Confirmation unlocks after reviewing the bottom, and a successful save dismisses setup across restarts.

### Changed
- Retention descriptions explain storage growth and the consequences of deleting each category. Existing keep-forever defaults and published Nextcloud recordings are unchanged.
