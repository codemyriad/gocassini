### Added
- Meetings whose summary was sent to the webhook receiver show a "Sent to {host}" chip with the administrator's optional reason, and Talk gets a short, silent second message when a meeting's summary will be sent. See "What people see" in `docs/events.md`.
- Webhooks panel: an optional "Why summaries are sent" reason (`summary_notice`) under `summary.completed`, with a preview of the notice. A receiver address must now have a plain host (`webhook_url_invalid`), so a summary is never sent without its notice.
