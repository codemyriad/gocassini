### Added
- Operator › Settings has a "Who can see recordings" section. It offers two audiences, one sentence each: "Everyone with a Nextcloud account" and "Meeting participants". The one in force is marked, and the line beneath says how many recordings already exist and who can see them, including after a switch, when the recordings from before it stay visible to everyone.
- Choosing "Meeting participants" on a Nextcloud that is missing Team folders or Everyone Group offers to install them, with Nextcloud's own Apps page beside it, and states that Everyone Group adds a group called Everyone to the whole of Nextcloud before anything is installed.
- A switch now shows its four steps while it runs, with a count of the recordings copied, and says the page can be closed. A switch that was interrupted offers "Resume".
- "Details for administrators" collapses the technical answers into one disclosure: where recordings are stored, which Nextcloud apps are in use, when storage was last checked and a link to the full report, what the `cassini` service account is for and how to set a password for it, and the same change as `occ` commands.

### Changed
- Confirming a switch now states what it means for new and for existing recordings in a number, in three sentences. Widening access back to everyone is the one confirmation that says the number in its own button.

### Removed
- The Setup tab's "Recording storage" card is gone. Every action it offered lives in the new settings section.
