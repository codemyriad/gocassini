### Changed
- Speech is now decoded with greedy search on every quality tier, on CPU and GPU, and participant names and the transcript vocabulary no longer bias decoding: the beam search that hints need dropped whole passages of speech, and keeping speech matters more than spelling names. `CASSINI_STT_HINTS_DISABLED` is now on unless set to `0`, which brings hints and beam search back for research. Search spellings work as before, and each transcript's provenance records that hints were not applied and why.
