### Added
- Switching storage modes now says what it would do before it does it. The confirmation used to state the policy — recordings will move, and their access rules will change — without any of the facts, so an administrator pressed the button and learnt the rest afterwards. It now reports how many recordings would move and between which folders, whether the destination already holds any, whether an earlier switch left a staging folder behind, and whether Nextcloud has renamed the archive out of the way. Working it out is read-only, and nothing happens until the change is confirmed. A preview Cassini cannot compute says so rather than showing an empty one.
