### Changed
- The MCP `list` tool reads meetings a page at a time (`limit`, default 25 and
  at most 100, and `offset`), and reports `total` so a page is never mistaken
  for the whole archive. Each entry is now a typed subset of the published
  catalog entry — id, title, date, room, speaker and segment counts, duration
  and whether it can be read — rather than the raw record with its storage
  paths.
