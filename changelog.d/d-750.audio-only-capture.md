### Changed

- New recordings capture audio only by default, including upgrades without a saved video choice. Administrators can opt into retaining source camera video using the unchecked **Capture video** choice presented during installation alongside storage retention, or later in Settings. Saving affects subsequent recordings; published meeting files remain audio only, and existing source video follows its retention policy.

### Fixed

- Preserve the same audio timing when source video is omitted, including single-track recordings remuxed with FFmpeg 6.
