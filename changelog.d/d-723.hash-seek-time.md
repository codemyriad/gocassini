### Fixed
- A meeting link's timestamp survives a trip through the Operator surface. The shell writes its own hash params — the selected run, and now the selected settings panel — after the viewer's `t=`, and the viewer only reads `t=` when it is the last thing in the URL. So opening a recording at a moment, stepping into Operator, and coming back left a link that still named the moment but no longer jumped to it. The shell now keeps `t=` last, the way the viewing layer already does when it writes a link of its own.
