### Added
- **Automatic setup of the recordings directory and its permissions.** When per-participant access control is enabled (`CASSINI_NC_ACCESS_CONTROL=true`), enabling Cassini provisions the `Cassini` Team folder, default-deny advanced ACLs, owner authority, and canonical `Cassini/Recordings/meetings` collections over Nextcloud's HTTP APIs—no manual `occ groupfolders:*` recipe. Every account receives a read-only mount through the virtual `everyone` group, while each recording remains private to its Talk audience or account-wide when its conversation was public. Administrators must first enable the Group folders (`groupfolders`) and Everyone Group (`group_everyone`) apps; the local harness installs both automatically.
