### Changed

- Upgraded Silero VAD to v6.3 and raised the speech detection threshold from `0.18` to `0.30`. Speech models now pin the new VAD revision on `dist.gocassini.com` while retaining the previous revision for retired stand-in models; on real multi-track meetings this preserves total word coverage, recovers more sub-second turns, and reduces background-noise audio sent to ASR.
