### Changed

- Current speech models now use Silero VAD v6.3 with a speech detection threshold of `0.30`. Retired stand-in models keep their previous VAD and `0.18` threshold. On three benchmark meetings, v6.3 produced 240 sub-second turns (up from 176) and reduced ASR time from 361 to 282 seconds.
- Existing installs need the new VAD before current speech models can run. Running `cassini models install` for the selected model reuses its installed speech weights. For an offline installation, import a model pack prepared with the updated catalogue.
