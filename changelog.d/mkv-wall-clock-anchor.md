### Added
- Recordings now carry a wall-clock anchor per audio track. Each track gains `first_packet_wall_ms` and `first_timeline_ns` MKV tags: two readings of the instant the track's first packet arrived, one in recorder wall time and one in meeting time. Because both come from the same monotonic clock, a later stage holding a wall-clock instant can convert it to a position on the meeting timeline exactly, without reopening the session artifact. The tags are emitted as a pair or not at all, and a recording that predates them reads as unknown rather than as the epoch. A third tag, `first_rtp_timestamp`, is recorded for triage only — Janus rewrites the timestamps it relays to each subscriber, so it is not the sender's clock and cannot place a sender-side time.
