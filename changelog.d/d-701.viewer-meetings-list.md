### Changed
- The in-Nextcloud viewer now reads its meeting list from the app's list endpoint rather than the published catalogue file, so a Nextcloud outage no longer empties the list: it keeps showing the meetings it last loaded and says what went wrong, instead of silently rendering as though the account had no recordings. Standalone exports and the static viewer are unaffected, and the viewer falls back to the catalogue file against an app that does not serve the endpoint.
