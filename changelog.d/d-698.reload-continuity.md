### Fixed
- **Reloading a Talk page mid-recording no longer costs the first half of what
  you said.** People reload most on bad connections, which is exactly when
  source capture is worth having. The page on its way out now seals its buffer
  and does not attempt an upload it cannot finish; the page that comes back
  resumes that buffer into the capture it starts, so one reload produces one
  capture holding both sides of the seam rather than two — or, before, sometimes
  one and a lost half.
- A buffered capture is never uploaded out of the directory a recorder is
  writing into. The page-load scan used to enumerate browser storage while
  uploading, so a capture starting during that scan could be sent half-written
  and then deleted.
- A capture whose manifest was truncated by a page dying mid-seal is recovered
  from its own recovery sidecar instead of being discarded.

### Changed
- **An uploaded capture is now spliced over the participant's recorded track
  rather than substituted for it.** Each segment replaces the recorded audio
  only across the window it actually holds audio for; a reload gap, a late
  start, or a segment that cannot be placed leaves the recorded audio exactly as
  it was. The recording remains the record — the published mix is still built
  from the recorded tracks, and both copies stay on disk to compare.

### Removed
- The 90% whole-call coverage refusal on uploaded captures. Whole-track
  substitution made an incomplete capture delete speech, and the threshold could
  only choose which participants that happened to — refusing, in particular,
  anyone whose page had reloaded. A splice cannot be worse than not ingesting at
  all, so there is nothing left to refuse. Per-segment checks stay: a segment
  that cannot be placed, or whose audio contradicts its own manifest, is left
  out and the recorded audio stands there.
