### Added
- An insight now keeps a record of itself from the moment it is asked for, moving through queued, running and then succeeded or failed. A run that takes minutes against a local model shows what it is doing rather than nothing, and a failed one says why it failed instead of stopping silently.
- Retrying a failed insight is another attempt on the same insight, not a second one: it keeps its id, workflow, meetings and question, and re-resolves the LLM provider and model from the current settings — so fixing a missing key or a wrong endpoint is enough to make the retry work. A retry pressed twice at once starts one attempt and refuses the other. A run the operator was restarted out from under is failed with a message asking you to retry it, instead of staying stuck on "running".
