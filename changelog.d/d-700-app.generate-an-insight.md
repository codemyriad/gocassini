### Added
- Generate an insight from inside the app. Pick meetings in the browse list, open **Prepare context**, and the panel now offers a Generate card under Copy and Download: choose one of the templates this deployment ships, or type your own question, and press Generate. The transcripts go to the deployment's configured AI endpoint and the answer is written into your own Nextcloud files.
- Insight runs report themselves honestly rather than as a spinner. A run appears in the card the moment you press Generate and carries `queued`, then `running`, then a result or a failure — a local model over five meetings takes minutes, and the card says so instead of implying seconds. A failed run names the cause in words you can act on ("No AI endpoint is configured", "The endpoint rejected the request"), carries **Retry**, and — for an administrator — links straight to the AI providers panel that fixes it. Retry re-resolves the endpoint and model from the settings as they stand when it is pressed, which is what makes adding a key a fix rather than a suggestion.

### Changed
- A non-administrator sees no template picker: the template registry is admin-only, so their insight runs whichever template the administrator configured, and the card says so rather than offering a control that would be refused. On a deployment with no AI endpoint the card does not appear at all — the existing "No AI endpoint is available" notice takes its place — and in a standalone static export, which has no operator to ask, neither appears.
