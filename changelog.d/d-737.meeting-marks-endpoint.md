### Added
- A meeting's tags and marks can be read and changed at `annotations/meetings/<id>`, by people in the app, the CLI and agents alike. Anyone who can read a meeting can mark it, and every mark is attributed to the Nextcloud account that made it, never to a name the request supplies. The marks are written into the recording itself, so they travel with it when it is downloaded or shared.
- Two people marking the same meeting at once no longer overwrite each other: a write that finds the recording changed underneath it re-reads the newer copy and applies its marks to that. A meeting you cannot read answers exactly as one that does not exist.
