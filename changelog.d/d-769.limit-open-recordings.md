### Added
- **Recordings a switch left visible to everyone can now be limited to the people who were in the call.** Choosing "Meeting participants" on an instance that already has recordings copies them into the Team folder and leaves them readable by every account — Cassini had no way of knowing who was in a past meeting, and said so once, in the page that performed the switch. It now keeps that record while each meeting runs: the accounts, groups and teams with access to the room are captured when a recording starts and again when it stops, and frozen, so a room that gains or loses members later cannot change who may open a recording made before the change. Operator › Settings › "Who can see recordings" lists everything still visible to everyone, shows exactly who each one would be limited to, and limits the ones you tick. Groups and teams are granted as themselves rather than expanded, so reorganising a team is how access moves with it.
- **Recordings it cannot limit say why, instead of sitting there unexplained** — recorded before Cassini kept the record, everyone in the call was a guest with no account to grant, or no job of this instance owns the file. Recordings of public conversations never appear at all: they are readable by everyone because the conversation was public. Anything needing a bespoke audience is still Files → Advanced permissions, and a recording you want left open can be dismissed from the list and brought back again.

### Changed
- **The warning about widening no longer overstates how permanent it is.** Switching to "Everyone with a Nextcloud account" said the recordings it opens will not be re-limited by switching back, which is still true and no longer the whole story: they can be limited again from the same settings section.
