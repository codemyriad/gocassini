### Changed

- CPU transcription at **Balanced** now uses Fluid Speech Pro (`fluid-speech-pro-0.6b-int8`), FluidVoice's fine-tune of Parakeet Ultra, quantized the same way as `parakeet-ultra-0.6b-int8-v2`. It transcribes English conversation more accurately (8.30% against 8.76% word error rate on AMI meetings, 2.83% against 3.13% on long TED talks) and matches it on German. It is less accurate on French (6.79% against 5.78%) and Spanish (4.72% against 4.21%), and slightly less on Italian. Speed and memory use do not change. If your meetings are mostly in French or Spanish, choose **Best** (fp32 Parakeet Ultra: a larger download, and slower on a CPU). Upgraded servers download the new model automatically (~465 MB) and transcribe with the previous model until the new one is ready (~640 MiB each on disk until Cassini removes the old folder). If your server has no internet access, run `cassini models pack` on a connected machine, then `cassini models import` on the server. If you roll back to an older Cassini release after the upgrade completes, you must download the previous model again. Fluid Speech Pro by FluidVoice. Built on NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 and Moondream Parakeet Ultra (CC BY 4.0).
