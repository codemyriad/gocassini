### Added

- Administrators can keep Nextcloud audio and meeting text for different periods, preview due effects and inspect recovery status. Both policies default to forever; unsupported storage and access-control configurations are refused.
- Audio expiry preserves a portable transcription document, annotations and existing Nextcloud file/share identities. Text remains searchable, readable and editable with playback disabled. Final expiry removes the active meeting and prevents Cassini from republishing it; Nextcloud still manages versions and Deleted files.
