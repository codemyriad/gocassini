### Added
- Pick several meetings in the Cassini app — across rooms, and the picks survive changing the room or the search — and Prepare hands you all of them as one document to copy or download. It is the same document `cassini meetings context` prints for the same meetings in the same order, byte for byte, because the app asks the same producer for it rather than assembling its own.
- Before it hands anything over, Prepare says what the bundle will not contain: how many of the picked meetings have no summary, how many were published before the index recorded whether they have one — a different statement, and never counted as the first — how many predate the single-file format, and whether the word count it shows is a total or a floor. All of it is read from the published meeting index, so nothing is downloaded to answer it.
- A meeting that leaves the archive while it is picked is removed from the selection and said so, rather than quietly shrinking the document you were about to hand over.
- A portable export offers no Prepare at all. There is no operator behind an exported site to assemble a bundle, and producing a lookalike in the browser would be a second implementation of a published format.
