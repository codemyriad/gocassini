Setup notices now lead with a visible browser setup button and keep server commands and diagnostics under “Technical details”. Choosing recording access is presented as a neutral setup step, including after upgrades, while existing recordings remain available. The setup invitation also works when only the public setup check reports that a choice is needed. The setup wizard describes options by who can see recordings, explains the instance-wide effect of Everyone Group, and keeps storage paths under a disclosure. Setup load failures now offer a plain-language explanation and a retry button, and the same recording-access names are used in the wizard and storage settings.
