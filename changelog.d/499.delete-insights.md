### Added

- An insight can now be deleted: open it and use the bin next to the pencil. Its document moves to your Nextcloud trash bin, and the meetings it was made from are not affected. Only the person who created an insight can delete it, and not while it is still running.
- An insight whose meetings were deleted, or are no longer shared with you, now shows **1 source unavailable** on its card in the list, so you can find it without opening it.
