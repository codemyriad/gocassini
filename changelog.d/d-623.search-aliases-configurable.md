### Added
- Administrators can tell Cassini the spellings transcription produces for names it gets wrong, in the app's settings beside the existing transcription terms. A search for the name then also finds the recordings where it was written down differently, and each result says whether it matched what you typed or a known mis-hearing of it. Cassini ships a starting list; anything configured here is added to it, and replaces it for a name that appears in both.
