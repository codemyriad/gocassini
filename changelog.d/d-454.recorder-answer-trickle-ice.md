### Fixed
- Talk recorder: stopped intermittent empty/blank recordings ("no remuxable streams found in session artifact", silent MKVs, 0-word transcripts). The recorder was holding its WebRTC SDP answer until ICE gathering fully finished, so a single slow or unreachable STUN/TURN server stalled the answer by the whole gather timeout — the media subscriber then reached ICE `connected` up to ~24s late (or was torn down before connecting), missed the call window, and captured nothing. The answer now goes out immediately with candidates trickled separately (standard trickle ICE), so a marginal ICE path no longer delays or drops the capture (D-454).
