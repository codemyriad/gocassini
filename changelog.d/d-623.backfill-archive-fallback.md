### Fixed
- `cassini-operator backfill-search` now indexes recordings this Cassini has no local copy of, by reading the published recording itself. Before, a meeting whose job record or working copy had been lost — which is every meeting published by an earlier installation, since the recordings outlive the app's own volume — was skipped entirely and silently absent from search. On the demo archive that was 25 of 30 meetings. Those are indexed at coarser granularity, and each result says which kind it is.
