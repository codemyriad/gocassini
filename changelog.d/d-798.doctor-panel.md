### Added
- Recording checks now live in their own **Doctor** section of the operator, instead of being buried in Publish pipeline settings. They run when you ask them to — there is a button to run them, and nothing checks in the background.
- Where Cassini can fix something itself, the check now offers a button that does it. Re-indexing the archive for search is the first: no command to copy, no terminal to find.

### Changed
- The checklist shows fewer rows, and only ones you can act on. Disk space, ffmpeg, ffprobe, the optional transcription row, the "no recent recording request from Talk" row and the test-recording row are gone; the recording volume's existence and writability are now one row. `cassini doctor` on a terminal still reports everything it always did.
- An archive with no meetings in it yet reads as fine, rather than as something unresolved.
