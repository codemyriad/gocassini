### Added
- `GET operator/setup`, which any logged-in Nextcloud user may read, now also reports what this deployment's AI configuration allows: `features.summaries` (a recording will be summarised) and `features.insights` (a question asked of a set of meetings will reach an endpoint). One bit each — never the endpoint, the model or the key, which stay administrator-only. It makes the promise in `docs/privacy.md` — that nothing leaves your infrastructure unless an endpoint is configured — checkable by the people whose meetings are being recorded.
- The Cassini app now explains an unconfigured deployment instead of staying quiet about it: picking meetings on an instance with no AI endpoint says so in the Prepare panel, and an administrator gets a link straight to the AI providers panel that keeps the meeting they had open. Anyone who is not an administrator is told the fact and that only an administrator can change it, rather than being shown a control that would be refused. The standalone viewer export, which has no operator to ask, says nothing at all.
