### Added
- You can tag meetings from the app. Each row in the meeting list shows its tags; tag one meeting from its row, or select several and tag them together. A filter beside the rooms narrows the list to one tag or several.
- Inside a meeting, a rail down the side of the transcript marks a stretch: drag it, then set exactly where the stretch begins and ends by dragging the markers in the text or with the arrow keys. Marked stretches appear as coloured brackets beside the words, and in a list that jumps to each one. You can also pick a tag first and mark several stretches with it.
- A tag has a colour and, if you want, an icon. You choose them where you create the tag, and change them in Manage tags, where tags can also be renamed, merged and deleted. A change reaches the meetings you can read; meetings in rooms you cannot open keep the tag as it was. Colours are stored for the installation rather than inside the recordings, so recolouring a tag rewrites nothing.

### Changed
- Finding words inside a meeting now counts the matches, steps between them, and marks where they are on the rail beside the transcript. Hiding the turns that do not match is now a switch, off by default, instead of what always happened.
