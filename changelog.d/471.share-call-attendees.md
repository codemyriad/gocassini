### Added

- A **Share recordings with** named choice in Operator › Publish pipeline: **Everyone in the Talk room** (the default) or **Only people who were in the call**. The attendee roster comes from Talk's in-call flag at recording start and stop plus every local account the recorder saw during the call, so late joiners and leavers are included and a standing channel's non-attendees stay out.
- The chosen audience is saved on the job at recording start and drives both captures, publication, the viewer label and Re-share; changing settings mid-recording or later does not rewrite an existing recording.
- In attendee mode the Talk room chat post is skipped, because the whole room would otherwise see the recording's title and link. If no attendee list was captured, the recording is shared only with the person who started it and the run shows a notice.
