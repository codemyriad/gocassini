### Added
- Host units for the unified meeting archive (`ops/cassini-archive-{sync,snapshot,healthcheck}.{service,timer}`, `ops/cassini-archive-sync.path`, `ops/cassini-archive-alert@.service`): the ingest runs three times a day, a nightly unit takes the read-only btrfs snapshot and re-hashes one integrity slice, and a watchdog runs twice a day. Every schedule is clear of the 03:30 off-host backup window and the monthly btrfs scrub, and every unit mounts both source corpora read-only so a bug cannot write, rename or unlink a recording.
- `ops/cassini-archive-healthcheck.sh`: fifteen assertions over the archive — last successful run, snapshot freshness, index-versus-tree agreement, stuck deferrals, free space, failed units, **timers that are no longer scheduled**, a finished ExApp capture that never reached the archive, and no new meeting for three weekdays. Exits non-zero naming the assertion that failed; `--json` for machine use.
- `ops/cassini-archive-alert.sh` plus `ops/98-cassini-archive-motd`: the `OnFailure=` handler for a host with no mail, no metrics and no messaging. It writes a durable, human-acknowledged `ALARM` marker, a `daemon.crit` journal record, a `wall` broadcast and a login banner, and stamps the failure into the off-host heartbeat so the nightly R2 copy carries it. An optional webhook or command notifier degrades to log-only when unconfigured and never fails the unit.
- `ops/README.md`: the exact host commands to install all of this, including the one `btrfs subvolume create` and the ownership that keeps the unprivileged container out of the archive.
