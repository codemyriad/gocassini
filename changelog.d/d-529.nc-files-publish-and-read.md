### Added
- The operator now delivers published recordings into **Nextcloud Files**. After a successful publish it uploads the meeting's portable `.opus` and the `catalog.json` index over WebDAV into a canonical `Cassini/Recordings/` folder, and the in-Nextcloud viewer's catalog + per-meeting audio are served from there (with the client, its CSP, and routes unchanged). Delivery is best-effort — it never fails a publish, and when Nextcloud is unreachable or a file is not there yet the viewer transparently falls back to the operator's local site. Recordings are delivered as `.opus` only; Talk's recording `/store` endpoint (which rejects `.opus`) is not used. This first pass is public and writes as the Nextcloud admin; per-user/per-group access and a dedicated service account follow.
