### Added
- The operator now delivers published recordings into **Nextcloud Files**. On startup and after each successful publish it synchronizes every portable `.opus` before updating the `catalog.json` index under the canonical `Cassini/Recordings/` folder. The in-Nextcloud viewer then serves its catalog and per-meeting audio authoritatively from Files (with the client, its CSP, and routes unchanged), while standalone development keeps its local source. Delivery remains best-effort and never changes a succeeded publish state, but failures are visible rather than silently masked by local fallback. Recordings are delivered as `.opus` only; Talk's recording `/store` endpoint (which rejects `.opus`) is not used. This first pass is public and writes as the Nextcloud admin; per-user/per-group access and a dedicated service account follow.
