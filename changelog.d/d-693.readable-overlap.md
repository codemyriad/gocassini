### Changed
- Overlapping speech reads as speech again. A turn is now one paragraph, however many pieces the producer cut it into: when two people talk over each other their sentences no longer alternate in one-to-three-word fragments. A short reaction someone made while another person was talking appears as a quiet chip where it happened, inside the sentence it landed in, and a turn that genuinely ran over another says so by naming the other speaker.
- Durations are gone from the transcript. A badge reading "0.2 s during Ivan" on every fragment was precision nobody acts on, and it made the page harder to read than no marking at all. The measurements are still there for anything that wants them; they are no longer shown.
