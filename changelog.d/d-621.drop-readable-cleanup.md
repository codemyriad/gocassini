### Removed
- The LLM readable-transcript cleanup step. Transcripts now publish as transcribed — with speaker attribution and paragraph breaks, but no LLM rewriting — and the only LLM step left is the meeting summary. Meetings that already carry a cleaned transcript keep showing it. Gone with the step: its configuration (`CASSINI_READABLE_DISABLED`, `CASSINI_READABLE_STRICT_BATCHES`, the per-step `READABLE_*` variables) and the preferred-spellings vocabulary setting, which existed only to steer the cleanup prompt.

### Changed
- Captions (`captions.vtt`) are now always produced, from the transcript itself; previously they existed only when an LLM endpoint was configured, as a by-product of cleanup.
