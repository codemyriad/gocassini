### Added
- Unified meeting archive (`ops/cassini-archive-sync.sh`): a host-side, root-only, additive reflink mirror that consolidates every Cassini capture era — the CT 107 cron recordings, the bare legacy `.mkv`, the Talk/Janus `hpb` recordings, and the Nextcloud ExApp `.run` bundles — into `/mnt/data/cassini-archive/`, with a stable `<anchor>--<room>--<era>--<slug>` identity, an `index.jsonl` row per meeting, read-only btrfs snapshots, and per-file sha256 verification. It never writes to a source tree and never deletes.
- Failure detection for the archive: every run writes `state/health.json` and an off-host heartbeat into the existing `cassini-exapp-backup` path list, a separate watchdog timer checks nine assertions independently of the ingest, and the run fails when no new capture has appeared for three weekdays — the silent stop that cost three unrecoverable business days in August 2026.
- `docs/meeting-archive.md`: the two recording eras and the 2026-08-03/04/05 handover hole, what "raw data before mixing" means (per-stream `.rtplog` vs. multitrack `recording.mkv` vs. the mono `amix` mixdown, with the measured ~59× collapse), the archive layout and naming, the backfill/sync/snapshot/health runbook and host install steps, the accepted single-copy exposure of the ~69 GB raw corpus, and the operational traps around root-only traversal, unprivileged-LXC uid mapping, and AppAPI-owned storage.
