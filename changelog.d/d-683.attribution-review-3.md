### Fixed
- A participant's noise floor is now the quietest floor any of their streams established, rather than a percentile over their pooled frames. Pooling was duration-weighted, so a long speech-only rejoin swamped the short stream that had actually seen the microphone idle, and crosstalk beat the real speaker again.
- Readable cleanup maps each rewritten word to the single best-overlapping source word, so one contradicted word can no longer flag a legitimate neighbour and delete it from the summary.
- The crosstalk badge now appears for portable meetings. Their display transcripts carry cleaned tokens rather than words, so the turn had nothing to be judged on and the badge never fired in the common case.
