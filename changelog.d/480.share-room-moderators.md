### Added

- A third **Share recordings with** choice in Operator › Publish pipeline: **Only the room's moderators**. Each recording is shared with the Talk room's owner and moderators, whether or not they were in the call; other members of the room receive nothing and no message is posted in the room's chat. The shares are read-only, so recipients cannot reshare the file.
- If Cassini could not read the room's moderators during the call, the recording is shared only with the person who started it, and the run shows a notice with **Re-share with moderators**, which reads the room's moderators then.
