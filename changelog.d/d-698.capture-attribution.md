### Fixed
- **A word recovered from somebody's own recording is no longer scored against
  the silence it replaced.** The crosstalk stage measured every speaker on the
  track the server received, even when their words had been transcribed from
  their own uploaded audio. Where the server received nothing, that read as a
  quiet participant under somebody else's voice — the exact signature of
  crosstalk — so the word was marked unreliable, kept out of the summary, and
  under the opt-in drop mode deleted, while being plainly audible in the
  published recording. It is now measured against the audio it came from, and a
  participant whose several tracks are covered by one upload is measured once
  rather than twice.
- **The manifest no longer says a participant's words came from their upload
  when the transcript was rebuilt from the mixed track.** When the
  per-participant pass comes back nearly empty, the whole meeting is re-decoded
  from the mix under a single "Everyone" speaker, and no word in the result
  carries any participant's name. Each entry under `provenance.sourceAudio` now
  carries `transcript_source`, which says whether the words that carry that
  speaker's name came from their own audio (`per-participant`) or from the mixed
  track (`merged-mix`); the build log says the same thing in the same words. The
  splice itself is still reported, because it is real: the published audio, and
  the mix that was transcribed, both carry it.

### Changed
- One participant's uploaded recording can no longer move everybody else's
  crosstalk verdict. An uploaded recording is continuous where the server's copy
  has holes, so its quiet baseline sits lower and every level on it measures
  further above that baseline. Measured on a synthetic three-speaker meeting, a
  10 dB difference was enough to make the stage stop flagging any crosstalk at
  all, silently, for every speaker. A participant whose audio was ingested is
  now held to the same usable range the meeting's other recordings establish.
  Meetings with no uploads, and meetings where everybody uploaded, are
  unaffected.
