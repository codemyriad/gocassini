### Changed
- "Who can see recordings" now has an **Always include the person who started the recording** option, on by default. Turning it off shares a recording only with the chosen audience; the starter still receives it when Cassini captured no audience. The choice is frozen on the recording when it starts.
- Attendee-mode recordings no longer always go to the room owner when the option is off, so an owner who did not join a call does not receive its recording.
