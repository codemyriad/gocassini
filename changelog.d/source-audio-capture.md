### Added
- Source-side audio capture, first stage: a participant's browser can now record its own microphone before Opus encoding and before the network, and upload it after the call. Cassini's recorder subscribes through the SFU, so until now the transcript could only ever be as good as each participant's uplink — words lost to packet loss were simply gone. The build can optionally transcribe a speaker from that audio instead of from what survived their uplink; ingestion is off unless an administrator sets CASSINI_SOURCE_AUDIO_INGEST, and a capture is only used when it comes from the same room and an overlapping call. Placement takes its rate from the participant's own encoded-frame timestamps — immune to packet loss, because those describe frames the client encoded rather than packets that arrived — and its offset from a wall-clock anchor the recorder now records per track. Offset accuracy is therefore bounded by how well the participant's clock agrees with the server's; a placement that falls outside the recording is refused, and every other failure leaves that speaker on the recorded track. Capture is off unless a user explicitly opts in, records the track Talk is sending (so Talk's own mute is honoured at the source and cannot be bypassed), and every upload is filed under the AppAPI-authenticated caller and refused for a room they were not in. The published audio stays the recorded mix — that is the meeting as everyone in the room heard it — and manifest.provenance.sourceAudio records which speakers were transcribed from their own capture and how well it fitted.
