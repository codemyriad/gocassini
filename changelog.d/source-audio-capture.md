### Added
- Source-side audio capture, first stage: a participant's browser can now record its own microphone before Opus encoding and before the network, and upload it after the call. Cassini's recorder subscribes through the SFU, so until now the transcript could only ever be as good as each participant's uplink — words lost to packet loss were simply gone. Captured audio is tagged with the RTP timestamps of the participant's own outgoing frames, which is the same clock the recorder logs for the packets that did arrive, so a later stage can place it on the meeting timeline even when the received audio is too damaged to correlate against. Capture is off unless a user explicitly opts in, records the track Talk is sending (so Talk's own mute is honoured at the source and cannot be bypassed), and every upload is filed under the AppAPI-authenticated caller and refused for a room they were not in. This stage receives and stores; placing the audio on the timeline and rebuilding the transcript from it is not wired up yet.
