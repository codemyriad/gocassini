### Added
- Proof of concept: separate the voices of people who shared one participant's device. In the app, a meeting's People panel offers **Separate voices** for a participant, then lets readers listen to each voice, name it, say two voices are the same person, or undo the split; the operator republishes the recording with the new speakers and rewrites its summary. Diarization runs locally on that participant's own track and stores only turn timestamps; nothing is re-transcribed and the audio is not re-encoded, so marks stay attached. The original transcript stays in the recording as `raw-asr`. See `docs/speaker-separation.md`.
- **Voice separation (optional)** in Settings: an administrator downloads the Nemotron 3 Diarization int8 model (about 62 MiB, CPU only) like any optional model; `cassini models install|pack|import` handle it too.
- `cassini speakers diarize|apply|show`, and the operator route `annotations/meetings/<id>/speakers` (GET/POST, for anyone who can read the meeting).

### Changed
- The privacy guide describes on-demand voice separation, the summary rewritten after a speaker edit (which sends the transcript and the typed names to the configured summary endpoint), and where names and editors are kept.
- Readers older than this release open only the default transcript of a separated meeting; switching to the `raw-asr` variant fails there.
