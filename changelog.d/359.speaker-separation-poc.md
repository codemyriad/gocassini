### Added
- Proof of concept: `cassini speakers diarize|apply|show` separates the voices of people who shared one participant's device. Diarization runs locally on that participant's own track and stores only turn timestamps; apply rewrites a built meeting from its original transcript without re-transcribing or re-encoding the audio, so marks stay attached. The original transcript stays in the recording as `raw-asr`. See `docs/speaker-separation.md`.
