### Changed
- Re-running a meeting no longer erases the tags and marks added to its recording after it was published: the rerun carries them into the recording it delivers. Marks made against audio the rerun changed are kept and flagged as no longer matching, never dropped. If marks are being written at the moment of the rerun, the publish fails and asks to be re-run rather than overwrite them.
- `cassini-operator backfill-search` recognises an archived recording that has gained marks since it was delivered as the same recording as the local copy — it compares the audio, not the whole file — and keeps indexing it from the local transcript's own segments.
