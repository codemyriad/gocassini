### Added
- The Doctor checks now report how much of your archive search can actually read — "129 of 138 meetings" — so a partial answer is visible instead of looking like no matches. A shortfall nobody can act on, such as a recording where nobody spoke, is reported without being counted against coverage.
- Where meetings are missing from the index because they were never indexed, the check offers a **Re-index now** button that does it, instead of a command to run.
