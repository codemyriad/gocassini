### Fixed
- Participant capture now stamps each recorded segment's window at the
  recorder's own boundaries — after `start()` returned, and before `stop()` was
  asked for — instead of before the recorder was constructed and after all the
  waiting that follows a stop. The old stamps declared the stopping as if it
  were audio, so a short final segment could claim up to a seventh more than it
  held and be left out of the splice although nothing had gone wrong. The
  seconds around somebody leaving a call were the least likely to come from
  their own microphone; they now splice like any other.
- Capture selection no longer lets a neighbouring recording's capture outrank
  the right one. A moderator who stops a recording and starts another in the
  same room produces two captures a minute of matching slack cannot tell apart;
  the capture whose call window genuinely covers a recording now wins, and the
  operator resolves an upload to its recording by the same rule instead of
  refusing the restart as ambiguous. Where two captures still cannot be told
  apart, the build refuses that participant with a named reason in the build log
  and keeps their recorded track, rather than guessing.
