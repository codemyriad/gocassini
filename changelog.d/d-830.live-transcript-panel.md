### Added
- Cassini Live gains a transcript panel for Talk calls: a scrollable, speaker-grouped live transcript that also shows your own lines, delivered as a bookmarklet and served by the cassini-live app (now a submodule) at `…/proxy/live_transcription/panel/`. It mounts as a "Live transcription" tab in Talk's sidebar, catches up late joiners (what was said before you joined, a "You joined here" divider, then the live continuation), and is scoped per conversation. No Nextcloud app changes are needed; captions still come from the cassini-live ExApp.
