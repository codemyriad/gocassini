### Added
- Cassini Live gains a transcript panel for Talk calls: a scrollable, speaker-grouped live transcript that also shows your own lines, delivered as a bookmarklet and served by the cassini-live app (now a submodule) at `…/proxy/live_transcription/panel/`. It mounts as a "Live transcription" tab in Talk's sidebar and shows the whole conversation — including what was said before you joined and anything you said before enabling captions — with a "joined" marker per participant. It is scoped per conversation. No Nextcloud app changes are needed; captions still come from the cassini-live ExApp.
