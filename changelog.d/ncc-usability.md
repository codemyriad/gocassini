### Added
- The selection bar's count opens a list of every meeting you have picked, including the ones the current room or search is hiding. Open a meeting from there, or unpick it.

### Changed
- Playback is shown in neutral rather than the accent colour: the word being played is inverted, and the play button, the scrubber and the Follow switch match it. The switch is called Follow at every width, turning it back on takes you straight to the audio, and the "Auto-scroll paused" badge is gone.
- A meeting reads with less around it: its details sit on two lines under the title, Summary, Insights and Transcript share one heading, and the transcript's search counts its matches and steps between them inside the field. The meeting always leaves a strip of the list beside it to click back to.
- The meeting list, the rooms rail and the Browse and Operator tabs follow the Cassini design. Dates name the day of the week, and who can open recordings is stated beside Rooms rather than among the filters.
- The Operator's sidebar is a drawer on a phone, and its settings pages sit on the page rather than in nested cards. First-run setup asks who can see recordings as a choice, and its problems read as warnings with a way forward.
- Prepare warns only about what stops it: a selection over the limit, or a meeting published in the older format.

### Fixed
- Keyboard focus moves into a meeting, an insight or Prepare when it opens and stays there: the page underneath can no longer be reached with Tab or read by a screen reader. Their close buttons are larger.
- Controls, borders, and hover and selected states stay visible in Nextcloud's high-contrast themes.
- The meeting and insight drawers stay readable in Nextcloud's high-contrast themes.
- Nothing in the list hides under the selection bar when the bar grows a second line, and an insights listing that failed reads as an error.
