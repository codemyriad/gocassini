### Changed
- A word can now be flagged `lowConfidenceSpeaker` only when its own track sits well below that speaker's own speech level (the quiet-owner signature). Genuine double-talk between microphones with different signal-to-noise ratios measures large gaps but is never flagged, and the crosstalk threshold is estimated on the quiet-owner population alone. The per-word `attributionGapDb` measurement itself is unchanged and still annotates every word.
- The meeting manifest records what the attribution stage did: whether it ran, the mode (annotate/drop/disabled), words measured, flagged and dropped, and the estimated threshold when one was found. A build that deletes words in drop mode is no longer indistinguishable from one that never saw them.
- `CASSINI_STT_BACKEND`, `CASSINI_ATTRIBUTION_DISABLED` and `CASSINI_ATTRIBUTION_DROP` are documented and passed through the deployment bundle; an unknown backend id now fails the build before any decoding or model download instead of after the mixdown.
