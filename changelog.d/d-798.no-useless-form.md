### Fixed
- On an installation with no High Performance Backend, the "Internal credential" check no longer offers a form to set a secret that cannot do anything yet. It explains what the credential is for, why it is not needed in this state, and points at the backend as the thing to fix first.
- Where the secret is needed, the check now says it must be pasted unchanged from the signaling server's configuration — a value that differs by one character fails exactly as though the credential were wrong.
