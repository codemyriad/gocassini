Cassini Recorder privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

We don’t know anything about you. The Cassini Recorder extension collects nothing, and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What the extension does

Cassini Recorder lets the Cassini app on your computer record the meetings you choose in your browser. While a meeting is being recorded, the extension passes the meeting’s audio, and the title of the meeting tab, to the Cassini app installed on the same computer. It uses your browser’s native messaging for this, which never leaves your computer. The Cassini app saves the recording in your own Music/Cassini folder.

What we collect

  • Nothing. The extension has no servers to talk to, and contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • Your recordings, transcripts and meeting titles stay on your computer. They are never uploaded by the extension.
  • We don’t sell, share or transfer any data, because we never receive any.

What stays in your browser

To keep a recording going if the browser restarts its background worker, the extension remembers which tabs are being recorded, in the browser’s session storage, which is kept in memory on your computer. It is deleted when the recording ends or the browser quits, and is never sent anywhere.

Changes

If this ever changes, we’ll update this page and its date before releasing a version of the extension that behaves differently.
