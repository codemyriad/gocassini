0.2.0 is the first stable release since the public alpha, and it includes everything from its alphas and betas. New since 0.2.0-beta.7: you decide how long Cassini keeps recordings, meetings can be published as transcripts without audio, and a Doctor page answers "will recording work?". If you run a beta, read *Before you upgrade* first.

### Highlights

**Decide what Cassini keeps, and for how long.** Operator → Storage sets how long each kind of file stays on the server: recordings, published meetings, previous attempts and processing logs. A separate setting does the same for published meetings in Nextcloud Files. A meeting there expires all at once: audio, transcript and notes. Cleanup runs once a day, at a time you choose. The Storage report shows how much space each kind of file takes, on the server and in Nextcloud Files, and which files the next cleanup will remove.

**Publish the transcript without the audio.** In Operator → Publish pipeline, set *What participants receive* to **Transcript only (.json)**. New meetings are then published as their transcript, with speakers, timing, summary and tags, and no audio. Under *What stays on this server* you can choose to keep nothing once a meeting is processed.

**Doctor tells you whether recording will work, and what to do if it won't.** One page checks Cassini's storage, Talk's High Performance Backend and its secret, and the connection to Talk. It walks you through a test recording and reports how much of your archive search can read. Each problem comes with the step that fixes it. When one problem stops the other checks from running, Doctor shows only that one.

**Talk links to each new recording.** Once a meeting is published, Cassini posts its link in the Talk conversation, as the moderator who started the recording.

**Separate voices that shared one device (preview).** When several people speak through one laptop, the meeting's People panel offers **Separate voices** for that participant. You can listen to each voice, name it, merge two voices into one person, or undo the split. Separation runs on your server and needs an optional 62 MiB model, downloaded in Settings.

### Before you upgrade

- **Nextcloud 33.0.9 to 35.** Nextcloud 32 is no longer supported.
- **Each recording is shared with the people in its Talk room**, through Nextcloud Files. This replaces the choice of who can see recordings that 0.2.0-beta.7 offered. Team Folders and the Everyone Group app are no longer needed.
- **Speech models are no longer in the image.** Download one in Settings, from dist.gocassini.com, and turn transcription on. Until then, recordings are published without a transcript, and Settings says so. A download that stops partway picks up where it left off, and downloaded models are kept across upgrades. Servers without internet access can use `cassini models pack` and `cassini models import`.
- **New recordings keep audio only.** To keep camera video too, choose **Audio and video** under *What stays on this server*.
- **Retention starts at keep forever.** Administrators see a reminder to review it in Operator → Storage. You can dismiss it.
- **Health monitoring** now uses `GET /operator/health`.

### Improvements

- **Meeting view:** Summary and Transcript tabs, a compact header, and one Export menu. Dragging along the timeline scrolls the transcript instead of selecting text.
- **Downloads:** transcripts as Markdown, the meeting's audio, and several meetings at once as a ZIP.
- **Insights** have names you can edit. You can export one as Markdown or open it in Nextcloud Files to share.
- **Smaller audio files:** meetings are encoded at the quality the call actually had. On our own meetings, files are about 30% smaller.
- **Settings:** one *After each meeting* card asks in order what happens to a recording: transcript, summary, what participants receive, and what stays on the server. A summary at the top shows the result.
- **Search results** keep the meeting's title and details readable at any width. Opening a result goes to the matching words in the transcript.

### Fixes

- Guests who join just as the recording starts are listed under their own name, not as `participant-<id>`.
- Tags and transcript marks appear as soon as you add them. If saving fails, you can retry.
- A recording embedded in another page no longer takes over the Space key, scrolls the page, or changes its address.
- Meetings keep the time they were recorded, even when transcription is off or fails.
- The Operator panels scroll to the bottom again.

### For integrators

- `cassini doctor --json` prints the checks as data, and each check has a stable id.
- The `<cassini-meeting>` embed has an inline layout and colour and font properties. It also sends a `playbackerror` event.

Every change is listed in the [developer changelog](https://github.com/codemyriad/gocassini/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
