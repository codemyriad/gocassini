0.3.0 makes transcripts more accurate by switching to a new speech model, Parakeet Ultra. Anyone can now import, rename and delete meetings, and ask for a missing summary. The upgrade needs more free GPU memory, so read *Before you upgrade* first.

### Highlights

**More accurate transcripts.** Cassini now transcribes with Parakeet Ultra, Moondream's retrained version of the Parakeet v3 model that 0.2.0 used. On a GPU, and on a CPU at **Best**, 0.2.0 could leave whole passages out of a transcript: about 250 words across 8 of our meetings. 0.3.0 keeps them, and on a GPU it takes about 40% less time. On a CPU at the default **Balanced**, it makes fewer errors. In exchange, it picks up slightly more of a loud neighbour's voice. Transcripts already published are not redone. ([How we measured](https://github.com/codemyriad/gocassini/pull/390))

**Import meetings.** Browse has an **Import** tab for the `.opus` recordings and `.json` transcripts Cassini lets you download, for example from another server or a backup. Keep each meeting to yourself, or share it with people, groups or Nextcloud Teams, who can share it further. Imported meetings appear in an **Imported meetings** room.

**Rename and delete meetings.** The pencil beside a meeting's title or a room's name renames it for everyone in Cassini. **Reset** and **Use default** bring back the original. The trash button deletes a meeting, or a selection of them, for everyone. That removes the recording, transcript, tags and the server's copy, and it cannot be undone. Insights that used the meeting keep their answer.

**Ask for a missing summary.** A meeting without a summary now says why. In the Cassini app, anyone who can open the meeting can click **Write summary**, if the server still has its copy. First, a confirmation shows the model, about how much text is sent, and whether it leaves your network. Administrators and the meeting's Talk room owners and moderators can also **Regenerate summary**.

### Before you upgrade

- **The GPU image needs NVIDIA driver 575 or newer** on the host, for CUDA 12.9. Data-centre GPUs can also use the 535, 550, 560, 565 or 570 branches. Check with `docker run --rm --gpus all nvidia/cuda:12.9.1-base-ubuntu22.04 nvidia-smi`.
- **Cassini downloads the new model on its own** (1.5 GB, or 465 MB on a CPU at **Balanced**; nothing at **Fast**) and keeps using the old one until it has it. On a server without internet access, run `cassini models pack` on a connected machine, then `cassini models import` on the server.
- **A GPU needs 7 GiB of free video memory**, up from 5.5 GiB. An idle 8 GiB card qualifies. A card that also drives a display or runs your summary model may not. Transcription waits for memory; if none frees up, the run in the Operator says so and you can rerun it. If you lowered this limit yourself, see *For integrators*.
- **A CPU at Balanced needs 3 GiB of free memory**, up from 2.75 GiB.
- **Names no longer steer transcription.** The *Transcript hints* vocabulary is gone, so names may be misspelled more often. Add the misspellings to **Search spellings** so search still finds them; that card does not change transcripts. If you turned hints on with an environment variable, see *For integrators*: it brings the dropped passages back.
- **Anyone who can open a meeting can rename or delete it, and rename its room, for everyone.** No setting restricts this. Restoring the file from Nextcloud's trash does not bring a deleted meeting back.
- **Anyone who can open a meeting can ask for a summary**, up to 10 an hour per person. Each request is a call to your summary provider, which may bill for it. Turning summaries off removes the button. Saving speaker names no longer rewrites a summary, unless an administrator or room moderator ticks **Also rewrite the summary for these names**.
- **Retention counts an imported meeting from the date it was recorded.** Check Operator → Storage before importing an old archive.
- **The App Store's Ethical AI rating for speech-to-text** goes from 🟡 to 🟠, because Moondream has not published how it retrained the model.

### Improvements

- **Link to a passage:** select part of a transcript and choose **Copy link**. The link opens the meeting with that passage highlighted, for anyone who can already see the recording.
- **Operator:** a run says why its meeting has no transcript or summary, and whether a rerun would help. Administrators can write a summary from a run without transcribing again.
- **Talk:** a new recording is announced with its room, date, length and speakers, not a bare link.
- **One-to-one calls** are titled with both names, such as "Alex & Chris".

### Fixes

- Tags added to several meetings at once appear immediately.
- The People panel can rename any participant, including one recorded as `participant-…`.

### For integrators

- `CASSINI_GPU_MIN_FREE_MB` defaults to 7168 (was 5500). A lower value you set can run the GPU out of memory.
- `CASSINI_STT_HINTS_DISABLED` set to `0`, `false`, `no` or `off` keeps the old decoding, which drops passages. Remove it.
- `CASSINI_SUMMARY_WRITES_PER_HOUR` sets the per-person limit (default 10; `0` removes it).
- The Operator's `/status` reports `stt.runtime_supports_model` instead of `stt.reference_frontend`.
- New commands and routes: `cassini-operator import-meeting`, `cassini meetings rename`, `cassini meetings summarize --estimate`, `cassini speakers apply --summary rewrite`, and `GET /operator/summaries/missing`.

Every change is listed in the [developer changelog](https://github.com/codemyriad/gocassini/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
