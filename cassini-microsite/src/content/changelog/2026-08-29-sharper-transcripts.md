---
title: "Sharper transcripts: interjections, long turns, and who said what"
date: 2026-08-29
version: 0.2.0-beta.6
---

A quick "yes" or "exactly" in the middle of someone else's sentence used to lose its position, its speaker, or both. Those land where they happened now, attributed to the person who said them, and near silent noise is no longer promoted into words.

Long turns are the other half. Speech running twenty to twenty five seconds without a break could come back with words missing. It is decoded in overlapping windows now, so the words survive, and where two windows disagree the transcript still has one clear owner for each word.

A meeting's length comes from the audio you can actually play, rather than from what the container claims.
