### Highlights

**Choose who receives each recording.** Admins can now narrow who receives a recording to **Only people who were in the call** or **Only the room's moderators**, under **Who can see recordings** in Operator › Publish pipeline. **Everyone in the Talk room** is still the default. With either of the narrower options, Cassini doesn't post the recording in the room's chat, so the rest of the room never sees its title. Moderators receive a read-only copy, in public rooms too. Each recording keeps the option that was set when it started.

**Cassini in your language.** The app, including Doctor, is now in French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Portugal), German (informal and formal), Dutch, Danish, Polish and Croatian as well as English. It follows each person's Nextcloud language. Dates and times follow their Nextcloud locale (Personal info › Locale). The translations start as machine drafts, which people are checking on Weblate.

**Meetings show when they really started.** Cassini used to show a meeting's start in UTC, whatever your time zone. Meeting times are now shown in your time zone, in the meeting list, search, insights and exported transcripts. Recordings made before the upgrade are corrected in the meeting list automatically.

**More accurate transcripts on a CPU.** At **Balanced**, Cassini uses a new, better-shrunk copy of its speech model, which keeps more of the full model's accuracy.

### Before you upgrade

- **Cassini downloads the new CPU model on its own** (about 465 MB, only on a CPU at **Balanced**) and keeps using the old one until it has it. Until it has it, the old model and the download together take up to about 1.7 GB of disk. On a server without internet access, run `cassini models pack` on a connected machine, then `cassini models import` on the server. If you go back to an older Cassini after the upgrade, download its model again.
- **Who receives recordings doesn't change** until you pick another option under **Who can see recordings**.
- **The start time inside past recording files is not rewritten unless you ask.** Operator › Doctor › **Recording times in past recordings** does it when you click **Write the recording time into** *N* **recordings**, with Pause and Resume. Before it starts, it says what a rewrite does on your server: each file gets a new version, and people the meeting is shared with may see a "file changed" activity entry. The meeting list is already correct without it.
- **The non-English languages are machine translations.** Anyone whose Nextcloud is set to one of them sees Cassini in it straight away.

### Improvements

- **Share email:** the note Nextcloud puts in the share email says the file is a Cassini meeting recording, with the meeting's name, date and length, and where to open it.
- **Download summary** in a meeting's Export menu saves the summary as a Markdown file.
- **Help:** the support bundle moved out of the strip above the meeting list. Everyone finds it under **Help** at the foot of the rooms list, next to **Connect an agent**. Administrators download the installation's bundle from the new Operator › Help page.

### Fixes

- A call where everyone was muted now says "No audio was captured. Were all participants muted?" instead of a generic error.
- Escape closes an open meeting, **Connect an agent** and **Manage tags** inside Nextcloud again.

### For integrators

- **Lifecycle webhooks (experimental):** an early feature that may change in any release before Cassini 1.0. Cassini can POST signed events (`recording.started`, `recording.stopped`, `recording.published`, `recording.failed`, `summary.completed`, `summary.failed`) to one receiver. `summary.completed` carries the summary as Markdown. Off by default; set it with `PUT operator/settings/webhook`. See `docs/events.md`.
- Meetings carry `recordedAtUtc` in `catalog.json`, the meeting list, search, the rooms list and MCP results. `dateLabel` is unchanged.
- `cassini meetings list --from/--to` are read in your local time zone; `published/meetings-list` and the MCP `list` tool accept instants with an offset.
- New: `cassini retag --recorded-at-utc`, `cassini inspect --meeting-times`, and `POST operator/migrations/recorded-at/start` and `/pause`.
- `/operator/status` sends `params` and step `id`s beside each Doctor message, so clients can word them in their own language. The English text is still sent.
- The `<cassini-meeting>` embed takes a `lang` attribute.
- The recorder exits with code 4 when no audio was captured.

Every change is listed in the [developer changelog](https://github.com/codemyriad/gocassini/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
