Try damaged live audio

Join a recorded call from the other tab. Use the same account to test two browser sessions, or different accounts for two participants. Switch to Media packet loss while speaking, then Restore normal before stopping recording.

Watch a late upload

Choose Slow recording upload before stopping recording. Watch the expected upload in Cassini from your regular browser. Restore normal to let it finish. Cassini waits up to two minutes, then late arrivals can trigger a rebuild.
