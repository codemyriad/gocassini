Questions

Does audio leave my device?

No. Recording, transcription and speaker analysis all happen on your phone or computer. The only download is the speech model, and only after you agree.

How much space does it need?

On a phone the speech model is a one-time download of about 530 MB. Allow about 1.3 GB free while it installs.

Is it free?

Yes. cassini.talk is free software under the GPLv3, and the source for every app is on GitHub.

Do I have to tell people I’m recording?

Yes. Before your first recording, cassini.talk reminds you that telling the people you record is your responsibility.

Is there a version for Nextcloud?

Yes. Cassini for Nextcloud records Talk calls on your own server and shares them with your team. It lives at gocassini.com, and it reads the same .opus files.
